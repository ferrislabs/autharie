Check that Herald is reaching the control plane:

  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} logs -l app.kubernetes.io/component=herald -f

A data plane only becomes selectable for new deployments once its first
heartbeat lands. Until then the control plane reports it as never seen, and
placement skips it.
On a fresh install, Herald and Genesis wait up to two minutes for the broker
to answer before giving up, so a cold cluster settles without restarts. A pod
that does restart after that wait is a broker it cannot reach, not a slow one.

pod restarts. Turn iggy.persistence on for anything beyond a throwaway cluster.
